How a match works

  1. 1

    Open a room

    Create a challenge in the app or join one waiting for a player. Your stake is held until the match ends.

  2. 2

    Play the match

    Add each other by EA ID and play an online friendly. A draw goes to penalties.

  3. 3

    Report the score

    Both players report the result with a photo of the final screen. The winner is paid automatically.
